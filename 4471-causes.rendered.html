The three problems

  1. The parser reads dates month first. Tenants that write the day first get flipped dates. This is the cause parser, and the largest group. Ticket: issue 1.
  2. The booking-v3 form prints a month-first hint. People at day-first tenants type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it their way. This is the cause hint, and only form reports show it. Ticket: issue 2.
  3. Some invites move by exactly one day. The desk read a few reports this way (see the note under the table). The date is not month-flipped, so the parser fix does not explain them. The cause is not yet investigated. Ticket: issue 3.

Why #4471 hid this

Intake tags a report from the reporter's browser, which is a guess. The cause depends on the tenant's locale and booking form, so the tag and the cause disagree (see the "tagged as" column). Merging by subject (decision 0001) never looks at either.

Lessons

  • Count by cause, not by subject. Merging early hides a second bug behind the first.
  • A ticket marked known should still say how many causes its reports hold.
